I’m a multidisciplinary builder, turning ideas into things you can see, feel, and interact with.

I’m Chenxin (Elle) Zhou, a multidisciplinary designer working at the intersection of design, technology, and business.

Born and raised in Shanghai and now based in Los Angeles, I’ve found that moving between the two cities has shaped my interest in creating products and experiences that connect people, ideas, and everyday life in novel, bold, yet meaningful ways.

I look closely at nature and everyday interactions, asking why things work the way they do and exploring how they could be different. Through hands-on experimentation, I develop these questions into products, with care for the people who use them and the environments they become part of.

I love asking why, connecting ideas across fields, and untangling messy problems until they make sense.

  • Product Development I develop products from initial research to working solutions, shaping form, function, and experience together.
  • Design Strategy I connect observations, question assumptions, and define directions with care for people and the environment.
  • Creative Technology I explore different mediums and technical approaches to find meaningful ways to address each problem.
  • Rapid Prototyping I test and refine ideas through sketching, Blender, 3D printing, Unity, and hardware prototyping.
  • AI in my practice I use AI to expand my capabilities and maximize efficiency while thinking critically and making deliberate, human-centered decisions.

Wood, fire, earth, metal, and water. The five phases of Chinese philosophy find patterns of change and connection in nature. I look to small details in nature to understand the larger world, bringing insights from different cultures into my design process to give ideas form.

01 Wood

震

Growth

Wood evokes the energy of new growth, reaching toward light with openness and possibility. To me, it represents a beginning, still undefined and free to grow.

Problem & Curiosity

My design process begins with curiosity. I observe, listen, and ask questions to understand people’s needs, finding clues in small details that reveal a meaningful design challenge.

02 Fire

离

Expansion

Fire evokes the energy of summer, rising and spreading outward, bringing light and warmth to everything it touches. To me, it represents expansion, an idea opening up to the world.

Research & Understanding

Once I find a challenge, I widen my view. Through interviews and secondary research, I try to see the whole picture, letting people’s stories and unexpected findings shape my understanding.

03 Earth

坤

Transformation

Earth sits at the center, holding and nourishing everything around it. It is where change quietly happens. To me, it represents transformation, turning what is gathered into something new.

Insight & Possibility

I turn research into insight. Using How Might We and What If questions, I reframe problems as opportunities and let possibilities multiply before choosing a direction.

04 Metal

兑

Refinement

Metal evokes the clarity of autumn, when the harvest is gathered and what is not needed falls away. To me, it represents refinement, shaping something precise and strong.

Focus & Prototype

I converge on one solution and give it form. Through prototyping, I cut away what is unnecessary, so the idea that remains is clear and can stand on its own.

05 Water

坎

Reflection

Water evokes the stillness of winter, flowing downward and settling deep, keeping what the year has given. To me, it represents reflection, the quiet before something begins again.

Testing & Iteration

I test my ideas with people, gather feedback, and reflect on what I learn. Each insight becomes the seed of a new question, and the cycle returns to wood.
